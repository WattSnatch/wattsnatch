# Commercial Use & Services

WattSnatch is source-available under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE). This page exists to answer the question the license text alone doesn't fully cover: **"who is allowed to charge money around WattSnatch?"**

## Free for personal use

If you're running WattSnatch to manage your own home's solar, EV charging, or hot water, you don't need anything from us. Install it, modify it, self-host it - that's exactly what the license permits, with no fee and no request required.

## Commercial use by third parties requires a license

If you want to build a business around WattSnatch that isn't you managing your own home - for example:

- Selling it, or a product that bundles it, for money
- Hosting it as a paid service for other people
- Offering paid installation, configuration, or managed-support services around it for other people's homes
- Using it as the basis for a competing commercial energy-management product

- that requires a separate commercial license from James Shafton, the copyright holder. Get in touch at **support@wattsnatch.app** to discuss terms - pricing and scope are decided case by case, so there isn't a fixed rate card here yet.

## Official WattSnatch services are not "third-party commercial use"

The clause above is aimed at *other people or companies* monetizing WattSnatch, not at the project itself. As the license holder, James Shafton / the WattSnatch project can offer official paid services directly - for example:

- **DIY** - free: source code, documentation, community support.
- **Assisted** - paid: a pre-configured mini PC / small server, shipped ready to plug in.
- **Premium** - paid: onsite installation and integration setup (Enphase, Tesla, Home Assistant, etc.).

These offerings may change or may not all be available yet - check [wattsnatch.app](https://wattsnatch.app) or contact support@wattsnatch.app for what's currently on offer. If you're a business that wants to resell or deliver similar services under your own name, that's the "commercial license" conversation above, not this one.

## Questions

If you're not sure which category your use case falls into, ask before you build on it: **support@wattsnatch.app**.
